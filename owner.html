Your vehicles.
Your updates.

Add a car, upload photos, change a price, or mark it sold. Your inventory editor handles the listings while the website handles the presentation.

Setup required

Connect the inventory editor

The vehicle editor is prepared, but the Sanity account and project still need to be connected. Online editing and publishing are not active yet.

Open inventory editor →
01 · ADD

Create a vehicle

Open All vehicles and create a new listing. Enter the year, make, model, asking price, and mileage.

02 · SHOW

Upload your photos

Open Photos and upload from your phone. Drag your best photo to the first position to make it the cover.

03 · PUBLISH

Put it on the website

Select Available, then Publish. Open the autos page to check your listing. Changes appear when that page is refreshed.

Everyday updates

  • Change a price: open the vehicle, update Asking price, and Publish.
  • Sold a car? set Availability to Sold and Publish. It leaves the autos page and stays in your editor.
  • Save it for later: keep it Hidden or unpublished until the listing is ready.
  • Check a listing: refresh the autos page after publishing. Verify photos, price, and mileage.
